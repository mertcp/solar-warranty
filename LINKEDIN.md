## Headline (≤ ~80 characters)

**Warranty Pulse — solar portfolio performance & OEM claim readiness**

## Short description (profile / project body)

I built a demo SaaS that helps solar operators see which sites sit below their output warranty, tie heat and missed inspections to the gap, and draft a defensible OEM review request—before the dispute becomes a spreadsheet war. FastAPI and SQLite on the back, a one-click portfolio UI on the front, optional LLM for the claim sentence. Synthetic portfolio only; built to explain cloud, API, and automation in pre-sales conversations.

## Highlights (use as one paragraph or split into lines)

- Portfolio ranked by **kWh below guarantee**, not vanity PR alone.  
- **Array view**: click a string; claim readiness and metrics load without an extra “generate” step.  
- **Evidence trail**: daily energy vs warranty floor, thermal stress, SLA dates—formulas visible.  
- **Human approval**: accept or edit the OEM draft; decision stored in the database.  
- **Integration story**: OpenAPI ingest for readings and milestones (`/docs`).
